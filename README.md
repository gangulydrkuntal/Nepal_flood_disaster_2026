# Nepal Floods 2026 — Geospatial Reconstruction

A geospatial reconstruction of the August 2026 Nepal floods and Langtang Lirung glacier collapse, combining pre-event terrain analysis with post-disaster imagery observations. Documents damage extent across 14 sites from the Nepal-China border to Nuwakot.

## Contents

| File/Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The story map. Self-contained story documenting pre- and post-event analysis. |
| `corridor_flythrough.mp4` | Pre-disaster 3D flythrough of the Bhote Koshi–Trishuli corridor. |
| `post_analysis/` | Post-disaster satellite imagery for 14 impact sites and imagery availability map. |

All files must be kept together — `index.html` references the video and post-disaster imagery by relative paths.

## Publishing on GitHub Pages

1. Push these files to the repository root (or to a `/docs` folder).
2. Repository **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Select branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
5. The site goes live at `https://<username>.github.io/<repo-name>/` after a minute or two.

## Sections

- **Pre-Event Conditions**: Analysis of terrain, glacier extent, and development patterns before the collapse.
- **Preliminary Post-Event Assessment**: Damage assessment across 14 sites with post-disaster satellite imagery showing buildings impacted.
- **How This Analysis Was Built**: Methodology for imagery cataloguing, cloud-free screening, and damage assessment.

## Data source and disclaimer

This study combines pre-disaster terrain analysis with publicly available post-disaster geospatial sources. Imagery analysis uses Google Earth and post-disaster high-resolution satellite imagery released through various sources.

**Analysis is pending due to limited availability of data sources.**

Distances, elevations, and geomorphic interpretations were derived from manual measurement and visual cross-checks. This is a reconnaissance analysis intended for informational and research use, not an official engineering assessment.
